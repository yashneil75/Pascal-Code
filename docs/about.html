Meet Simply

Your new assistant, and the app that keeps it running on your side of the screen.

Simply is the assistant that lives inside Simpld. Open the app, pick a model from the built-in library, and Simply loads it onto your GPU and answers right there in the window. No server does the thinking: the model is the main character, and it lives on your disk.

Because everything runs on hardware you own, your code, your files and your questions never leave your machine. Replies come back fast and the internet can be switched off entirely. If the router dies mid-sprint, nothing about your workflow changes.

Simpld is open source and costs nothing. The model library carries several tiers, from small files that run on almost any machine to full-size weights for capable GPUs, so you only download what your hardware can lift. Grab a build on the download page and simply start.
